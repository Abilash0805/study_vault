Questions students ask

How do I get a study material on Chapter Kit?

Sign in with Google and open the store. You can buy a single chapter outright, or take a weekly or monthly plan that opens every material on the site. Payment is by UPI.

What does a plan include?

A plan opens every study material on Chapter Kit, including ones published while the plan is running. The weekly plan is ₹69 for 7 days; the monthly plan is ₹199 for 30 days.

Do I lose my materials when a plan ends?

No. Any chapter you bought on its own stays in your library for good. Only the plan's extra access stops, and there is no auto-renewal, so nothing is charged again.

Can I see a sample before paying?

Yes. Every new student can ask for one free sample from the store, and your instructor sends a material across so you can judge the teaching before buying anything.

What can I read the materials on?

Any device with a browser — phone, tablet or laptop. Nothing is downloaded or installed, and Chapter Kit can be added to a phone's home screen so it opens like an app.

How do I pay?

By UPI. Checkout shows a QR code and a payment link with the amount and reference already filled in, and access is granted once the payment is confirmed.
